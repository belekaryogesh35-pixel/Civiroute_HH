What is CiviRoute?

One platform for traffic + civic road intelligence.

CiviRoute is designed around vehicle-mounted cameras on moving vehicles. AI analyzes captured road imagery to detect vehicles and infrastructure conditions, attaches location information, stores observations in a backend database, and presents insights through dashboards and applications.

🚗

Moving-Vehicle Vision

Vehicle-mounted cameras continuously capture road scenes while the vehicle travels through the city.

🤖

AI Detection

Computer vision can identify cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks and selected road-condition issues.

📍

Geotagged Intelligence

Observations can be linked with location, time, image evidence and road context for traceability.

Core capabilities

Built for real urban problems.

A single dashboard can connect field observations, AI analysis, citizen reports and repair workflows.

🚦

Traffic Monitoring

Vehicle counting, traffic-density estimation, congestion indicators and route intelligence.

🕳️

🗑️
